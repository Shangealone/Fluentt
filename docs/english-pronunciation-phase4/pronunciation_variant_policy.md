# Draft English pronunciation-reference policy — Phase 4

**Status: proposed, not active.** A qualified English phonetics reviewer and research adviser must approve the reference inventory, acceptable variants and intended feedback before any scoring change. This document does not amend the current WavLM scorer.

## Reference contract

1. For each fixed English prompt, store its exact display text, normalized token sequence, edition/version, pronunciation-lexicon identity and checksum, selected baseline phone sequence, and reviewer/date. Keep this separate from a learner's recording and rating. A correction creates a new edition; historical predictions retain their original reference.
2. Begin candidate lexical entries with the **installed CMUdict 0.7a** (SHA-256 `cad209c39eb87677d64e93d97f8eed10b7e6f9bdd42de8e7ca8efc8e17d62e8a`). CMUdict's ARPABET vowel digits encode lexical stress. The deployed `g2p-en` 2.1.0 path selects its own output, then `pipeline.py` strips all digits and maps ARPABET symbols to the alignment model's IPA tokens. The actual saved model reference, not a dictionary guess, is the research comparator. Document any G2P fallback or out-of-vocabulary name for manual review. Do not silently add a generated pronunciation as approved.
3. Maintain a **reviewed variant register** per word and context: full stressed phones, stress-stripped phones, lexical source/version, variety and evidence, scope (e.g., function-word weak/strong context), decision, reviewer and date. Dictionary presence establishes a documented lexical alternative, not automatic acoustic acceptability in a given sentence. Human review may approve a documented rhotic or non-rhotic realization, reduction or full vowel, or Philippine English variant when intelligibility and target construct support it. No global accent correction or arbitrary substitution list.
4. For a proposed acceptable variant, distinguish (a) the phonological analysis, (b) alignment/tokenization compatibility, (c) whether the **trained head** remains calibrated on that expected sequence, and (d) whether raters accept it. These are separate checks. A Philippine English study describes real rhoticity and vowel-reduction variation in a limited Metro Manila sample; it does not license a universal Philippine English phone mapping.
5. Unknown word, uncertain name, unsupported phone, failed alignment, ambiguous variant, missing prompt metadata or low-quality audio: report **unable to assess** where necessary and invite a retry or human review. Never replace an unknown with a plausible-looking numerical score, mark a dictionary mismatch as a learner error, or use the expert-selected SpeechOcean reference as a runtime oracle.
6. Preserve both raw and displayed reference text; normalize text only for an explicitly versioned comparison, with original punctuation, casing and apostrophes retained for review. Do not merge/split words silently. Record token differences separately from phone differences.

## Current implementation boundary

`PronunciationAssessorV2._text_to_phonemes()` calls one `G2p()` sequence for each token; it strips stress and `_arpabet_to_model_idx()` chooses the first available IPA alias in a fixed mapping. It does **not** score several candidate references, compare alignments across variants, estimate variant likelihood, or restore stress. Multiple-reference acceptance would require separately reviewed design, implementation, development data, latency/memory checks and untouched-speaker validation. This policy is documentation only.

## Review queue from the 30-recording pilot

See `pronunciation_reference_mismatches.csv`: 37 distinct word positions. Its `observed_pattern` is a mechanical comparison, while `pronunciation_acceptance` stays **UNRESOLVED** on every row. Four show a pure written `r` difference; 21 one vowel-symbol replacement; five one segment insertion/deletion; one multiple vowel replacements; four mixed/consonant replacements; two complex differences. There are **zero stress-only differences in this inventory by construction**, because the original comparison strips stress digits. Stress remains unassessed by the model. The proper-name `LAYLA` has no entry in this installed CMUdict; review its name-specific pronunciation rather than relying on G2P.
