- User clarification (2026-10-29): the materials are inspiration and teaching references, not Fluentt's complete syllabus. ## Proposed pilot lesson map

The added collections below are original draft content informed by the registered source topics. They have not been reviewed or published.

### SRC-01 — Grade 3 conversation and everyday words

Source: SRC-01

- Topic: Greetings, food, shopping, time, routines, actions, and places.
- Learner outcome: ask and answer short everyday questions with confidence.
- Starter activities: hear-and-choose vocabulary, build a short question/answer sentence, flashcards for key words.
- Suggested difficulty: Easy, with a small Medium follow-up after review.
- Status: Seven original lessons imported; see `LEARNING_MATERIALS_COVERAGE.md`.

### Pilot B — Early phonics and beginning sounds

Source: SRC-03

- Topic: Alphabet names, letter sounds, and familiar beginning-sound words.
- Learner outcome: connect letters to sounds and identify the first sound in a simple word.
- Starter activities: hear-and-choose letter/word, build a short sound-word phrase, flashcards for letter and picture-word pairs.
- Suggested placement: separate early-reader lesson group; do not mix with Grade 3 conversation lessons.

### Candidate follow-up groups

- SRC-02: separate Grade 4–5 units such as school clubs, places in town, health, and habits.
- SRC-04: Four original reading-skills lessons imported with new passages; see `LEARNING_MATERIALS_COVERAGE.md`.
- SRC-05: Grade 1–2 short reading units from School, Ice Cream, and Houses after the client confirms the pages and permitted wording.
- VID-01–VID-07: older-learner grammar and conversation units; keep them separate from the Grade 3 and early-reader tracks.
- VID-08: optional resource-literacy material for older learners or teachers, not a core learner activity.

### SRC-05 chapter map from the client’s screenshots

- Chapter 1 — **School**, Lessons 1–4, pages 6–31: school supplies, school places, classroom shapes, and different school environments.
- Chapter 2 — **Ice Cream**, Lessons 5–8, pages 32–57: counting and quantities, food concepts, ownership, ingredients, and simple instructions.
- Chapter 3 — **Houses**, Lessons 9–12, pages 58–83: household vocabulary, types of homes, storytelling, and animal habitats.
- Supporting activity patterns: key words, read and circle, read and stick, comprehension, “your turn,” and wrap-up tasks.
- Supplementary projects: Shapes at School, At the Ice Cream Shop, and My Dream House.
